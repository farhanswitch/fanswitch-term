# Projects

## **PT Inovasi Solusi Internasional - Backend Developer (2022 - Present)**


During my time at PT Inovasi Solusi Internasional, I have been involved in designing and developing several business-critical applications, ranging from enterprise trading platforms to AI and blockchain-based solutions.

One area that I have focused on extensively is system modernization. I led the migration of Regol and Eform, two core platforms used by one of Indonesia's largest Forex trading groups, from PHP/Laravel to TypeScript and Express.js. Through this modernization effort, I was able to reduce Regol's off-peak resource consumption by approximately 75% while also improving the overall maintainability of the system. For Eform, I addressed multiple security vulnerabilities identified through penetration testing and implemented a dynamic Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) system that automatically masks sensitive document information based on each user's access level.

Later, I joined the Etrade team, where I participated in rebuilding a large-scale trading platform into a microservices architecture. One of the most interesting challenges was that the original vendor did not provide access to the source code, which required us to reverse-engineer the existing system and carefully reconstruct its functionality. During this project, I optimized several data aggregation processes, reducing response times from more than 30 seconds to approximately 1 second. I also contributed to transforming a legacy desktop reporting application into a modern web-based platform that is easier to maintain and accessible across different devices.

Beyond backend systems, I am also interested in emerging technologies. I developed a Dynamic Object Detection System using YOLOv8 that supports real-time inference from multiple video sources, including CCTV streams, server-side cameras, and client webcams. This allows users to switch between video inputs seamlessly without interrupting the detection process.

In addition, I built a Blockchain Payment Gateway designed to provide a payment experience similar to QRIS for cryptocurrency transactions. The system supports USDT payments across multiple blockchain networks, including Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, and Tron. It includes dynamic fee management, automated transaction verification, and secure on-chain validation to ensure reliability and accuracy throughout the payment process.

Looking back at these projects, I believe they have strengthened my ability to design scalable backend architectures, solve complex technical challenges, and deliver solutions that create measurable business impact. At the same time, they have given me the opportunity to work across different domains, from enterprise systems and financial technology to artificial intelligence and blockchain applications.
